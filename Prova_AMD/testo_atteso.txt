Testo atteso (due parlanti: A voce normale, B voce piu' grave). Serve solo a confrontare la trascrizione.

A: Good morning, everyone. Thanks for coming in on such short notice.
B: No problem. What's going on? You sounded worried on the phone.
A: We lost the Henderson account last night. Three years of work, gone.
B: You're kidding me. They signed the renewal in September, didn't they?
A: They did, but their new director wants to cut costs by thirty percent.
B: Thirty percent? That's ridiculous. We can't match that without firing people.
A: I know. That's why I called you. I have an idea, but you're not going to like it.
B: Try me. I've had worse news before breakfast.
A: We move the whole team to the new office in Boston and rent out this building.
B: Wait, wait. Boston? My kids are in school here. I can't just pick up and leave.
A: Nobody is asking you to leave tomorrow. We have until the end of March.
B: Fine. But I want it in writing, and I want a raise if I say yes.
A: Done. I'll talk to the board this afternoon. Can you send me the budget by noon?
B: I'll have it on your desk in an hour. Don't worry, we'll figure this out.
A: I hope so. If this goes wrong, we're all going to be looking for a new job.
B: Hey, look at me. We've survived worse. Remember the flood in twenty nineteen?
A: How could I forget? We worked for two weeks with no power and cold coffee.
B: Exactly. So a few spreadsheets won't kill us. Come on, let's get to work.
A: Thanks. Really. I don't know what I'd do without you.
B: You'd probably burn the place down. Now go, you're going to be late.
